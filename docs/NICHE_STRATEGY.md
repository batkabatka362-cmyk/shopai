## 2. Three niche positioning options

### Option A — "Calm & Cozy" (RECOMMENDED)

**Positioning:** *Turn your bedroom into a 5-star hotel spa.*

**Hero products (11 of 20):**
- Galaxy Star Projector Night Light
- Galaxy Projector Lamp
- 3D Moon Lamp Night Light
- LED Sunset Lamp Projector
- LED Cloud Light
- Rain Cloud Humidifier with LED
- Sunrise Alarm Clock Light
- Crystal Aromatherapy Oil Diffuser
- Anti-Gravity Humidifier with Clock
- Smart Aroma Diffuser
- Essential Oil Gift Set (6 Pack)

**Drop / hide (6 of 20) — don't fit persona:**
- Magnetic Phone Mount for Car ❌ tech/commute, not cozy
- Desktop Wireless Charging Pad ❌ office, not bedroom
- Smart Water Bottle Temperature Display ❌ fitness, not wellness
- Portable Neck Fan Bladeless ❌ summer commute
- Smart LED Strip Lights RGB ⚠️ borderline — could go "gaming room" not "spa"
- Portable Mini Projector HD ❌ entertainment, not mood

**Keep cluster-neutral (3):**
- Floating Shelf Set
- Magnetic Levitation Floating Plant Pot
- Minimalist Desk Organizer

**Target customer:** Women 22–40, urban apartment, sleep struggles, anxiety/burnout, wanting "home sanctuary" aesthetic, TikTok + Pinterest users.

**Meta Ads angle:**
> *"Your bedroom should feel like a vacation — not an alarm clock. Soft galaxy light + calming aromatherapy = the 5-star hotel treatment at home."*

**ROAS potential: HIGH.**
- Impulse price point ($15–$35)
- Emotional hook (sleep, stress, self-care) — proven Meta converter
- Visually stunning products = scroll-stopping video ads
- Gift-worthy (Mother's Day, Christmas, birthdays, housewarming)

### Option B — "Magic Home" (wow-factor decor)

**Positioning:** *Items that make every visitor ask "where did you get that?"*

**Hero products (5):** Galaxy Projector Lamp, Magnetic Levitation Plant Pot, 3D Moon Lamp, Floating Shelf Set, Anti-Gravity Humidifier

**Drop (15):** Everything else — too many supporting products don't reinforce "magic" theme.

**Weakness:** Only 5 hero products → catalog feels thin. Would need 10–15 more AliExpress items.

**ROAS potential: MEDIUM.** Wow-factor converts well but needs more SKUs.

### Option C — "Smart Home Essentials" (tech focus)

**Positioning:** *Smart gadgets that make everyday life 10× easier.*

**Hero products (6):** Smart Water Bottle, Smart Aroma Diffuser, Smart LED Strip, Wireless Charging, Mini Projector, Magnetic Phone Mount.

**Drop (14):** Ambient lighting + aromatherapy items.

**Weakness:** Highly competitive Meta Ads niche. Amazon dominates. ROAS harder.

**ROAS potential: MEDIUM-LOW.** Saturated.

## 6. What won't work (honest)

- **Expecting $10K/month in month 1** — unrealistic. Even optimised stores need 4-8 weeks ad-training.
- **Tech gadgets as lead niche** — Amazon + Shein dominate + Meta CPCs too high.
- **Product range > 40 SKUs** — splits customer attention, hurts brand clarity. Stick to tight 15-20 focused SKUs.
- **Skipping Shopify Payments** — without checkout working, even best ads produce 0 orders. Priority 1 owner blocker.
